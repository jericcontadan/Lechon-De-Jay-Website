Our Story

Lechon De Jay is a young Filipino food business owned by TJ Yuson. It opened on July 5, 2026 in Casimiro Westville, Ligas 2, Bacoor City, Cavite, with a physical store for dine-in and takeout.

Mission

To serve authentic Filipino lechon and dishes for everyday meals and big celebrations, with reliable service, while supporting the community around us.

Vision

To grow Lechon De Jay into a trusted, well-known Filipino food brand with stores in many places.

Community

The ₱99 budget meal supports the neighborhood and builds trust; it is not meant to earn profit. We also support local youth and sports groups.

Franchising

Lechon De Jay is open for franchising, with a goal of 100 stores in ten years.
